Every dataset starts with a first observation. This is mine.

I've wanted a place to write for a long time; somewhere between the rigor of a notebook and the looseness of a diary. Posts here will roughly fall into three buckets:

1. **Analysis** - write-ups of things I've dug into: TidyTuesday datasets, Kaggle competitions, questions that started with "I wonder if…"
2. **Books** - notes on what I'm reading, because Goodreads stars never say enough.
3. **Notes** - shorter, half-formed things. Ideas caught before they escape.
